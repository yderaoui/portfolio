# Anime space artwork

Generated with the built-in image generation tool, then refined with the same tool for transparent-background use.

Final asset: `public/franxx/zero-two-space-v1.png`.

Layout and motion reference: [Space Themed Website Design and Animation by Dannniel for Marcato Studio](https://dribbble.com/shots/8071472-Space-Themed-Website-Design-and-Animation). The reference animation is not embedded or redistributed in this project.

Character-identity reference: the official Zero Two illustration in `public/franxx/zero-two.png`; see `public/franxx/SOURCES.md` for source credits. The new illustration is AI-generated fan artwork.

## Generation prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: transparent anime character cutout for a web hero inspired by an ink-drawn space collage. Use the attached official Zero Two character image only as a character-identity reference, not as an edit target. Create a fresh full-body illustration of Zero Two from Darling in the Franxx drifting weightlessly through space, a dynamic diagonal pose with her head at the upper left and her boots extending toward the lower right, knees loosely bent, one arm reaching outward and one bent near her chest. Recognizable long pastel-pink hair, small red horns, green eyes, calm confident expression. She wears a fully covering white and charcoal science-fiction pilot suit with a few vermilion-red armor panels, boots and gloves, no helmet. Hair and suit straps flow gently upward to communicate zero gravity. Style: exquisite Japanese anime and manga illustration, precise black ink outlines, controlled crosshatching and screen-tone shadows, mostly white and charcoal with selective soft pink hair and red accents. Crisp editorial illustration, hand-drawn, no photorealism, no 3D rendering, no chibi proportions. Composition: entire character including hands, hair and boots visible, generous transparent margin on all sides, silhouette reads beautifully at small size, a thin clean ivory cutout edge. Portrait canvas. Background must be genuinely transparent. No planets, no stars, no lettering, no text, no symbols, no logos, no watermark, no UI. Attractive cinematic composition, tasteful and nonsexual.

## Final refinement prompt

Edit the supplied illustration. Preserve the anime character exactly: her face, pink hair, red horns, pose, white/red pilot suit, ink lines, boots, and the thin ivory outline. Remove the ENTIRE smoky brown/gray/black background and all ambient shadow, glow and vignette surrounding the character. This is a production website sticker asset: every pixel outside the character silhouette and its thin outline must have alpha exactly 0. Keep the character itself fully opaque. Do not include any background color or gradient, not even translucent haze. Do not alter, repaint, crop, redesign, or reposition the character. Return a clean RGBA transparent background cutout.
